Industrial Grade Standards

Commercial Durability. 100% Locked Security.

Unlike cheap unprogrammed tags bought online that fail when wiped with bar sanitisers or get overwritten by customer phones, VenuePoint hardware is built for rigorous commercial operations:

Permanent Write-Locking

Hex-level lock prevents malicious tampering or accidental overwriting by any smartphone.

IP67 Water & Spill Proof

Epoxy resin and sealed acrylic resist heavy beer spills, commercial glasswashers, and rain.

Dual NFC + QR Redundancy

High-contrast laser printed backup QR ensures 100% compatibility across all device generations.

3M VHB Industrial Adhesive

Anti-theft structural bonding keeps pucks and cards firmly affixed to timber, marble, and metal.
