Revenue Optimization

Why Contactless NFC Menus Drive Higher Average Order Value

Physical print menus limit sales by hiding images, restricting upsells, and creating server bottlenecks. Here is the operational math behind why NFC table touchpoints generate measurable revenue uplift.

+23%

Visual Appetite Stimulation

Digital menus display high-resolution photography and video of signature dishes, cocktails, and desserts. Visual appetite cues trigger high-margin impulse dessert and cocktail additions that text-only print menus fail to inspire.

Point-of-Choice

Automated Upsells & Pairings

Servers forget to suggest sides or premium wines during a busy rush. Digital menus automatically prompt: "Add truffle parmesan fries (+£3.50)" or "Pair with Argentinian Malbec (+£8.50)" right at peak buying intent.

Zero Latency

Faster Second-Round Drinks

When drinks run dry, guests hate waiting to flag a busy waiter. With instant NFC tap access, guests can browse the cocktail or craft beer list immediately, turning what would have been a 1-drink table into a 2 or 3-drink table.

£0 Reprints

Live Specials & Margin Control

Change prices, add high-margin chef's specials, or instantly 86 out-of-stock items in seconds without paying £500+ for emergency laminate reprints. Protect your gross margins across every single shift.
