Clear Workflow

How It Works

A simple, dependable process from initial product selection to live digital deployment.

Step 01

Choose Product

Select stands, cards, stickers, or keyrings that fit your physical space and business use case.

Step 02

Define Destination

Specify the web URL, Google review profile, digital menu, or social account you want customers to reach.

Step 03

We Configure

Where agreed, we program NTAG microchips and pair dual-read high-contrast backup QR codes.

Step 04

Test & Deliver

Every piece is verified on iOS and Android devices, then dispatched or installed on-site where arranged.

Step 05

Customer Taps

Diners, visitors, or event attendees hold their phone near the touchpoint and reach your destination instantly.

Beyond Just Supplying Boxes

Professional Programming & Venue Installation

Many suppliers simply drop a box of unprogrammed stickers in the post. VenuePoint offers end-to-end commercial support:

Everyday Applications in Action
