Operational Understanding

Your venue. Better connected.

The modern hospitality experience extends far beyond the food, drinks and attentive table service. Today's guests increasingly expect immediate, convenient access to menus, straightforward review channels, and effortless digital interactions.

When customer touchpoints are disjointed or difficult to navigate, venues lose momentum. We implement discreet, durable digital touchpoints throughout your venue—at entrance points, counters, and dining tables—making guest interactions seamless, intuitive, and completely frictionless.

Implementation Process

A structured approach to venue technology.

We handle every stage from site survey to post-installation support, ensuring zero downtime for your service staff.

Step 01

Understand the venue

We review your layout, guest flow, service style, and existing systems to identify where digital touchpoints deliver genuine operational value.

Step 02

Design the solution

We specify the exact physical materials, mounting styles, and digital landing destinations that complement your interior design and brand.

Step 03

Configure & install

Touchpoints are programmed, tested across iOS and Android hardware, and neatly installed without disrupting active service hours.

Step 04

Support & improve

We ensure destinations remain current, links function flawlessly, and the system scales smoothly as your menu or operations evolve.

Tailored Implementations

Built for the realities of UK hospitality.

Every hospitality environment has distinct operational constraints. We adapt our touchpoint specifications to suit your service rhythm.

Independent Pubs Pubs & Bars

High bar volume and busy garden tables demand robust, waterproof touchpoints that direct guests to quick drink menus and Google review pages.

  • Spill-resistant table and beer garden discs
  • Countertop Google review displays
  • Instant allergen and taplist access

Restaurants & Bistros Dining

Elevate the guest journey with discreet, elegant menu access and post-meal feedback touchpoints that preserve table aesthetics.

  • Architectural acrylic or timber finishes
  • Wine list and seasonal specials navigation
  • Bill-presenter review integration

Cafés & Bakeries Counter Service

Reduce queuing friction during morning rushes by placing digital menu displays at order lines and pickup counters.

  • Counter-mounted menu cards
  • Daily rotating pastry & coffee boards
  • Takeaway review prompts at packaging stations

Multi-Site Operators Groups

  • Centralised coordination & rollout support
