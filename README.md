# VenuePoint — Premium UK Hospitality Digital Solutions Platform

A complete, multi-page website built for **VenuePoint Hospitality Solutions Ltd**, a UK-based B2B technology provider serving independent pubs, restaurants, bistros, cafes, bars, and multi-site hospitality groups.

---

## Brand Positioning & Operational Focus

- **Core Positioning:** *"We help hospitality businesses create better-connected customer experiences through practical digital solutions."*
- **Primary Audience:** Independent pub owners, restaurateurs, cafe operators, hospitality directors, and multi-site operations managers across the United Kingdom.
- **Philosophy:** Technology should make hospitality simpler, not more complicated. Focused on practical, durable physical touchpoints (NFC and QR) that connect venues directly to Google reviews, live digital menus, and table ordering with **zero app downloads required** for guests.

---
