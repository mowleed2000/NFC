Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

VenuePoint Hospitality Solutions Ltd ("VenuePoint", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data. This privacy policy informs business clients and visitors about how we look after personal data when visiting our website or engaging our hospitality technology services.

2. Data We Collect

When you contact us or request a venue assessment, we may collect:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, professional role.
  • Contact Data: Business email address, business telephone number, venue address.
  • Venue & Operational Data: Number of sites, hospitality venue category, table capacity, point-of-sale platforms in use.
  • Technical Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system when visiting our website.

3. Customer Dining Interactions (End-User Guests)

A core design philosophy of VenuePoint is consumer privacy. When diners tap or scan physical touchpoints in client venues:

  • We do not require diners to create accounts or install apps.
  • We do not track personal browsing histories across third-party websites.
  • When accessing Google reviews, diners interact directly with Google LLC under Google's own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Business Data

We process business contact information strictly for legitimate commercial interests: providing project quotations, conducting venue site surveys, fulfilling installation agreements, and delivering customer support.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain business enquiry data only as long as necessary for contractual and regulatory purposes under United Kingdom law. We employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to safeguard your information.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of your personal data. To exercise any rights, contact us at privacy@venuepoint.co.uk.
