Structured Rollout Framework

From One Venue to an Entire Estate

We do not expect you to commit an entire estate on day one. Our structured rollout framework begins with a controlled pilot before scaling across your portfolio.

Stage 01

Estate Strategy & Survey

We review your estate size, service models, and customer engagement objectives, followed by on-site surveys of representative venues.

Stage 02

Controlled Pilot Deployment

Deploy a full installation across 1–3 selected locations. Gather real feedback from general managers and floor staff over a 30-day trial.

Stage 03

Review & Refinement

Analyse guest tap rates, review conversions, and hardware durability. Fine-tune physical placements and digital destinations.

Stage 04

Estate-Wide Rollout

Execute structured multi-site deployments with dedicated installation teams and pre-configured hardware batches per venue.
