Our Mission

Turning Physical Interactions into Digital Actions.

VenuePoint supplies and configures physical NFC products that connect businesses, customers, and event audiences to the digital experiences that matter.

Our Approach

Practical Products. Zero Customer Friction.

In an increasingly digital world, businesses still conduct their most valuable interactions in physical spaces: across dining tables, over retail counters, during conference handshakes, and at trade exhibitions.

Yet bridging that physical moment to a digital destination has traditionally been clumsy—relying on forgotten paper flyers, misspelled URLs, or demanding that customers download proprietary mobile apps.

VenuePoint eliminates that barrier. We provide commercial-grade NFC stands, cards, stickers, keyrings, and table markers that launch verified digital destinations natively in a customer's smartphone in under two seconds.

Commercial Scope

The Businesses We Support

We work across several commercial sectors, adapting hardware and programming to each environment.

Hospitality Venues

Independent pubs, restaurants, bars, and multi-site groups needing Google review capture, live digital menus, and table-side convenience.

Event & Conference Organizers

National exhibitions, trade shows, and networking events ordering high-utility promotional keyrings and smart attendee badges.

Retail & Service Businesses

Shops, salons, dental and aesthetic clinics, and trade professionals capturing direct customer reviews and social media follows.

Agencies & Promotional Companies

Marketing agencies and brand consultancies sourcing custom-encoded merchandise and white-label NFC campaign collateral.
