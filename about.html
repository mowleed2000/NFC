Our Philosophy

Practical Technology. Better Hospitality Experiences.

We believe technology should make hospitality simpler, not more complicated. We focus on practical digital improvements that connect physical venues with convenient customer experiences.

Focus & Expertise

Why we focus exclusively on hospitality.

Hospitality is a demanding, high-intensity industry. In a bustling pub or full-capacity dining room, staff have seconds to spare, and guests have zero patience for glitchy software, forced mobile downloads, or clunky logins.

Too many tech providers treat restaurants like software companies—imposing complex dashboards and ongoing maintenance overheads. At VenuePoint, we build backwards from the realities of the floor: touchpoints that are completely waterproof, tamper-resistant, visually unobtrusive, and genuinely frictionless for diners.

By focusing exclusively on pubs, bars, restaurants, and hospitality groups, our hardware designs, destination software, and installation methodologies are tailored specifically to UK licensing, food allergen standards, and guest dining behaviours.

Quality Commitments

Our installation and support standards.

Every venue project is executed to uncompromising commercial standards.

Zero Service Disruption

All physical installations are scheduled during prep hours or closed mornings. We never drill, test, or position hardware during live trading.

Commercial Durability

We use industrial 3M commercial adhesives, marine-grade acrylics, and UV-sealed print layers that withstand aggressive cleaning chemicals and pint spillages.

Universal Hardware Testing

Before leaving your premises, every installed touchpoint is tested across Apple iPhone and Android handsets to verify instant NFC reading and QR clarity.

Direct Accountability

No overseas call centres. When you need assistance or a menu destination updated, you deal directly with experienced UK technicians who know your venue.
