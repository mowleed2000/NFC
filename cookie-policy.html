Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They help websites remember preferences and provide aggregated usage analytics.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for core website functionality, security, and remembering your cookie consent preferences.
  • Analytical Cookies: Help us measure anonymised traffic patterns and popular pages so we can improve our website navigation and content.

3. Controlling Cookies

You can choose to disable or block cookies through your individual browser settings at any time. For more information, visit aboutcookies.org.
