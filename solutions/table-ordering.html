Explore Table Ordering
The 7-Step Flow

From table tap to kitchen preparation.

A transparent, structured workflow designed to keep kitchen, bar, and floor staff in perfect sync.

Step 01

Access Menu

Customer taps or scans the table marker to open the live venue menu.

Step 02

Select Items

They choose dishes, sides, and beverages with optional modifier notes.

Step 03

Confirm Table

The touchpoint automatically pre-fills their exact table identifier.

Step 04

Submit Order

Diner reviews the basket summary and confirms order transmission.

Step 05

Secure Payment

Payment is processed via Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card in seconds.

Step 06

Order Received

Tickets route to your supported kitchen printer, KDS, or bar terminal.

Step 07

Food Delivered

Floor staff deliver freshly prepared items directly to the correct table.

Compatibility & Integration

Designed Around Your Existing Operations

We do not believe in forcing hospitality venues into expensive, proprietary software ecosystems or making false claims of "universal plug-and-play POS compatibility".

Every restaurant, pub, and bar operates with specific point-of-sale hardware, merchant acquirers, and service protocols. Technology should work alongside your staff—not fight against your daily rhythm.

During our initial assessment, we audit your existing POS, order workflow, and payment gateway to advise whether direct API integration, parallel tablet routing, or hybrid ordering delivers the smoothest operational outcome for your team.

  • ✓ No forced card terminal replacements
  • ✓ Optional hybrid mode (table drinks reordering + traditional floor service)
