Make Every Customer Interaction Count.

Capture spontaneous, authentic feedback from satisfied guests before they leave your premises. Our physical review displays connect diners directly to your Google Business Profile in seconds.

Discuss Review Solutions
The Experience

A frictionless four-step review journey.

We remove every hurdle between a happy diner and a published review on Google.

Step 01

Notice the display

Discreet, professionally branded touchpoints are placed where diners naturally pause—such as bar counters, table corners, or bill folders.

Step 02

Tap or scan with phone

Customers simply hold any modern smartphone near the touchpoint or scan the crisp backup QR code. No app or camera download is required.

Step 03

Review page opens

The smartphone browser immediately opens your venue's verified Google review form, bypassing manual searching, typos, or competitor listings.

Step 04

Guest shares feedback

The customer voluntarily chooses star rating and writes their comments while their positive experience is fresh in their mind.

Touchpoint Options

Built for heavy commercial hospitality use.

Constructed with scratch-resistant, water-sealed acrylics and solid timber mounts engineered to withstand spillages, regular sanitising, and busy shifts.

Countertop Displays Bar & Till

Weighted, freestanding stands positioned at the till or bar pickup. Perfect for grabbing feedback while guests wait for drinks or settle their tab.

Table-Based Touchpoints Dining

Ultra-low-profile pucks or integrated table number blocks that keep tables neat while giving diners access at the end of their meal.

Entrance & Exit Touchpoints Lobby

Wall-mounted or lectern displays positioned at reception, coat check, or exit doors to capture parting impressions.

Bill-Presenter Discs Table Service

Slimline inserts designed to sit inside traditional leather bill presenters, presented alongside the receipt when guests are paying.

Google Policy Compliance & Honest Feedback

Google's review guidelines strictly prohibit incentivised reviews, selective "gating" (filtering unhappy customers away from public reviews), or fake submissions.

VenuePoint solutions connect guests directly to your official Google review form without intercepting or manipulating the customer journey. This ensures 100% compliance with Google policies, preserving the integrity and longevity of your venue's business profile.
