Solutions Portfolio

Practical Digital Solutions for Hospitality Venues

We bridge physical hospitality venues with modern digital convenience. Our installations deliver clear operational value without requiring complex hardware overhauls or customer app downloads.

Reputation & Visibility

Review Solutions

Business Purpose: Help genuine satisfied guests find and access your verified Google review profile with zero friction, capturing authentic feedback at the right moment.

How It Works: Discreet countertop, table, or bill-presenter touchpoints trigger your official Google review submission interface directly on the guest's smartphone via NFC tap or QR scan.

Suitable For: Independent pubs, restaurants, bistros, cocktail lounges, and multi-location venues focused on local search prominence and organic reputation building.

Frictionless Access

Digital Menus

Business Purpose: Eliminate menu reprint expenses, maintain real-time allergen and dietary accuracy, and allow guests to browse complete food and drink offerings instantly.

How It Works: Customers tap or scan durable table or counter touchpoints to open responsive, fast-loading digital menus within their mobile browser—no app installations required.

Suitable For: High-turnover dining rooms, rotating seasonal menus, extensive craft beer and cocktail bars, and outdoor patio spaces.

Self-Directed Service

Table Ordering & Payments

Business Purpose: Reduce service bottlenecks during peak hours, increase secondary drink orders, and give guests the freedom to reorder and pay at their own pace.

How It Works: Table-specific touchpoints identify the diner's exact table number, allowing them to select items, pay securely via Apple Pay or credit card, and send orders directly to your supported kitchen display or bar terminal.

Suitable For: Expansive pubs, beer gardens, casual dining establishments, and sports bars experiencing peak rush periods.

Holistic Deployment

Complete Venue Installation

Business Purpose: Create a smooth, unified journey across the guest visit—from menu preview at entrance, to table-side access, to departure feedback.

How It Works: We conduct a comprehensive site survey, design matched architectural hardware touchpoints, configure secure destinations, and professionally install every unit with zero operational disruption.

Suitable For: Venue refurbishments, new site openings, multi-room pubs, and operators wanting a single accountable installation partner.
