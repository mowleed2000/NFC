Discuss Digital Menus
Operational Benefits

Why venues switch to smart digital menu access.

Eliminate the friction, expense, and waste of frequent physical menu reprinting.

Instant Updates

Update 86'd items, rotate weekend specials, and adjust prices instantly across every table in seconds without reprinting a single sheet.

Flawless Allergen Compliance

Meet UK food information regulations with searchable, clear ingredient breakdowns and allergen filters that give diners complete confidence.

Zero App Barrier

Over 80% of customers abandon menus that demand an App Store download. Our touchpoints launch instantly in Safari and Chrome via native NFC or QR.

Cleaner Tabletop Presentation

Replace worn, sticky laminated menus with minimalist, elegant timber or acrylic markers that elevate your dining room ambiance.

The Guest Flow

Simple, natural diner interactions.

Step 1: Sit Down

The diner sits at their table and notices the discreet menu touchpoint.

Step 2: Instant Access

They tap their phone or scan the high-contrast code with their camera.

Step 3: Browse Naturally

Your beautiful, responsive menu loads instantly with high-res imagery, prices, and tasting notes.
