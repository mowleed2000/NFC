Pilot Project 01

The Crown & Thistle Pub

Independent Gastropub & Large Garden | Surrey, United Kingdom

Deployment: Review Displays & Garden Table Menus

The Operational Challenge

The pub experienced heavy outdoor footfall across 32 garden tables during sunny weekends. Floor staff were constantly ferrying paper menus that frequently blew away or suffered rain damage. Concurrently, despite high customer satisfaction, the pub rarely received Google reviews from dining patrons.

The Installed Solution

Mo surveyed the garden and fitted waterproof, UV-resistant table pucks to all 32 outdoor tables, programmed with the live daily menu and allergen sheet. Two weighted timber Google review displays were positioned on the main bar counter next to the till point.

Operational Observations

Garden diners accessed menus instantly upon sitting down, reducing initial server wait times by an estimated 3–4 minutes per table. Countertop review displays captured spontaneous reviews during payment pauses without staff feeling uncomfortable asking for feedback.

Next Steps

Extending table ordering touchpoints to the furthest perimeter garden benches to support peak afternoon drink re-orders.

Pilot Project 02

Olive & Ember Bistro

Contemporary 45-Cover Italian Bistro | London, United Kingdom

Deployment: Complete Dining Room Touchpoint Suite

The Operational Challenge

A weekly rotating tasting menu created recurring print costs and frequent last-minute corrections when specific wine vintages or market seafood items sold out before evening service.

The Installed Solution

Installed discreet dark walnut timber table markers on each dining table, paired with bill-presenter NFC review inserts. The digital menu was linked to their web CMS, allowing head chef and sommelier to modify dishes and vintage pairings with a 30-second phone update.

Operational Observations

Zero printed menu waste achieved across 8 consecutive weeks. Diners appreciated having detailed sommelier tasting notes accessible on their phones while still enjoying personalised floor hospitality from waiting staff.

Next Steps

Preparing pilot rollout for the owner's sister trattoria opening later this season.
