Hospitality Pilot

The Crown & Thistle Pub

Gastropub & Large Garden | Surrey, UK

Products: Review Displays & Table Menu Pucks

The Challenge

Paper menus in the 32-table beer garden frequently blew away or were ruined by rain. Concurrently, despite busy weekend trade, the pub rarely received Google reviews.

The Solution Installed

Mo surveyed the garden and mounted 32 IP67 waterproof timber menu pucks to the tables, linked to their live daily menu. Two white L-shaped Google review stands were placed on the main bar till points.

Operational Observations

Diners accessed menus immediately upon seating, reducing initial wait times. Bar staff noted steady organic Google review growth without having to awkwardly ask patrons.

Events & Exhibitions

TechInnovate Annual Conference

Annual Tech Summit (450 Attendees) | London, UK

Products: 450 Custom Epoxy NFC Keyrings

The Challenge

The organizer wanted to eliminate 40-page printed event booklets while providing a high-perceived-value physical giveaway that attendees would retain after the event.

The Solution Supplied

VenuePoint produced 450 double-sided branded black-and-white epoxy NFC keyrings with split rings. Each chip was encoded with the dynamic mobile event web-app containing live stage schedules and sponsor links.

Operational Observations

Zero printed booklets needed. Over 90% of attendees tapped their keyrings during the 2-day conference, and organizers reported high positive feedback on sponsor link engagement.
