The Unit Economics

How Contactless NFC Delivers Measurable Multi-Site ROI

Physical NFC touchpoints are not an overhead expense — they are a high-margin operational asset that increases guest spend and drives high-intent local search visibility.

+18% to +26% Average Order Value (AOV)

Diners seated at tables with instant NFC menu touchpoints browse rich food imagery and high-margin cocktail menus without waiting for a server. Point-of-selection pairing suggestions and frictionless second-round beverage re-ordering consistently boost gross cheque size.

£8,000 – £25,000 Annual Print Savings

A 20-site restaurant chain typically spends thousands each quarter reprinting laminated food and cocktail menus whenever supplier ingredient costs or wine vintages shift. With NFC dynamic redirects, price changes happen instantly at zero print cost.

Dominant Google Maps Footfall

By placing dedicated NFC Review Displays at host stands and providing servers with NFC keyrings, branch review volume increases 5x–10x. When tourists or locals search "best pizza near me" or "casual dining nearby", your branches rank in the top Google Maps 3-Pack.
