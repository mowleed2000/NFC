NFC Cards, Stickers & Tags

Reliable, industry-standard physical NFC hardware. From blank programmable NTAG cards to durable outdoor coin tags and adhesive vinyl stickers.

Request Hardware Quote Explore Components
Product Formats

Physical NFC Hardware Options

We supply verified microchips compatible with all NFC-enabled modern smartphones.

Programmable NFC Cards

Credit-card-sized CR80 PVC cards in matte black or glossy white. Blank or custom-printed. Ideal for digital business cards, VIP passes, and access keys.

  • Dimensions: 85.5mm x 54mm x 0.84mm
  • Available in NTAG213, NTAG215, NTAG216
  • Writable and password-lockable

Round NFC Coin Tags

Rigid 30mm ABS plastic coin tags with center hole or flat adhesive backing. Designed for asset tracking, inventory check-in, or equipment inspection.

  • Impact and weather resistant
  • Anti-metal shielding layer available
  • Long service lifespan

Adhesive NFC Vinyl Stickers

Ultra-thin adhesive NFC labels. Apply under glass, behind signage, or inside product packaging to turn any physical surface into an interactive tap point.

  • 25mm and 35mm round formats
  • Paper, PET, or waterproof vinyl finishes
  • High-tack permanent adhesive

NFC Coasters & Table Discs

Natural oak, dark walnut, and thick acrylic round discs. Engineered for restaurants, pubs, and hotel lounges for drinks menus and guest WiFi.

  • Diameter: 90mm, thickness: 5mm
  • Waterproof resin-sealed NFC core
  • Laser engraved branding available

Device Compatibility Guidelines

NFC background tag reading is supported natively on iPhone XS and newer (iOS 14+) and all modern NFC-equipped Android handsets (Android 8.0+).

For maximum real-world reliability, VenuePoint recommends pairing NFC touchpoints with high-contrast printed backup QR codes to ensure 100% of visitors can access your digital destination regardless of phone model or age.
