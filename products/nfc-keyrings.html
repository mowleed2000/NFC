Product Specifications

Engineered for Daily Carrying

Constructed with durable epoxy resin and premium zinc alloy split rings to survive pockets, bags, and daily car keys.

Circular Epoxy Keyrings

30mm diameter domed epoxy keyrings. Scratch-resistant, IP67 waterproof, and lightweight. Perfect for high-volume conference giveaways.

  • Chipset: NTAG213 / NTAG215
