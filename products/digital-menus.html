Why Contactless NFC Menus Drive Higher Average Order Value

Physical print menus restrict sales by hiding dish photography, omitting upsell prompts, and creating order latency. Here is how frictionless NFC touchpoints increase guest spend.

High-resolution food and cocktail photos stimulate cravings that text-only print menus fail to trigger, driving higher dessert and appetizer orders.

Automated Upsells & Pairings
