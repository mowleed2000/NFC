Technical Capabilities

Configuration, Programming & Venue Installation

One of our key commercial differentiators is that we offer more than a box of NFC products. We configure, verify, and physically deploy customer engagement touchpoints across the UK.

Discuss Your Deployment

Important Service Distinction

Bulk Promotional Orders (e.g. Keyrings, Cards, Stickers): Generally involve physical product supply and agreed batch programming before dispatch. They do not include on-site visits.

Venue Installation Projects (e.g. Pubs, Restaurants, Hotels): For suitable hospitality and multi-site projects, our services can include comprehensive layout surveys, bespoke surface mounting, and on-site testing.

Service Breakdown

Our Core Technical Services

Tailored delivery options depending on your technical capabilities and operational scale.

1. Product Supply

Reliable sourcing of commercial-grade acrylic stands, domed epoxy keyrings, rigid PVC cards, and industrial NTAG microchips. Unprogrammed blank units available for in-house developers.

2. NFC Chip Programming

Where agreed, we encode NTAG microchips with your verified web destinations, Google review profiles, or social handles, and password-lock the chips to prevent unauthorized modification.

3. Dual QR Code Configuration

We pair every touchpoint with a laser-etched or high-contrast printed backup QR code, ensuring 100% customer access even on older handsets without active NFC.

4. Universal Handset Testing

Prior to on-site fitting and handover, every batch is verified on Apple iPhone (iOS) and Android devices to guarantee instant native reading without awkward placement delays.

5. Physical Placement & Mounting

For UK venue projects, our lead specialist Mo surveys table traffic, bar counter queues, and lighting, securely mounting touchpoints with industrial adhesives or tamper-resistant fixings.

6. Multi-Location Deployments

For pub estates and retail chains, we coordinate nationwide batch deliveries mapped to each branch's specific Google Business Profile and local menu URLs.

Structured Process

The VenuePoint 6-Stage Delivery Model

A proven, transparent methodology ensuring zero guesswork and zero operational downtime.

Stage 01

Consultation

Review requirements, quantities, locations, and digital destinations.

Stage 02

Product Selection

Select the physical form factors, materials, and mounting specifications.

Stage 03

Configuration

Program NTAG chips and format paired dual-read backup QR codes.

Stage 04

Testing

Verify tap speeds and link destinations across multiple mobile operating systems.

Stage 05

Delivery / Fitting

Delivered and professionally installed on-site across UK venues by our specialist technicians.

Stage 06

Handover

Provide destination documentation and operational guidance for floor teams.
