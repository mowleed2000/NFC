Seven Small Digital Improvements That Can Make a Difference in a Restaurant

You don't need a complete technological overhaul to modernise your venue. These seven practical improvements deliver outsized operational results.

Restaurant operators are constantly bombarded with expensive software suites promising to revolutionize their business. In reality, marginal gains across daily customer touchpoints produce far more reliable return on investment.

1. Dedicated Countertop Review Displays

Placing an elegant, weighted review display at the payment till captures positive reviews when customers are happily settled and waiting for payment receipts.

2. Digital Tap Lists on the Bar

Rotating guest craft beers, ciders, and seasonal draughts on a tap-linked digital menu spares bartenders from reciting six brewery names to every customer.

3. Bill-Folder Touchpoints

Adding an ultra-thin NFC insert inside bill presentation wallets lets diners leave a voluntary Google review while the bill is being processed.

4. Instant Allergen & Dietary Filters

Giving guests with dietary requirements the ability to filter gluten, nuts, and dairy independently on their smartphone eliminates anxiety and saves waiting staff 5 minutes per table.

5. Entrance Window Menu Previews

A weather-resistant touchpoint on your entrance window allows evening pedestrians to browse your full menu and prices after closing hours.

6. Secondary Drink Reordering Pucks

Enabling simple beverage reordering at perimeter tables and outdoor gardens increases wet sales without requiring additional floor runners.

7. Simplified WiFi Onboarding

Replacing complex multi-page captive portal forms with a one-tap connection card that delivers fast guest WiFi without password frustration.
