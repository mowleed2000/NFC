How to Introduce New Technology Without Disrupting Restaurant Operations

Hospitality teams are busy and often skeptical of new gadgets. Here is how to introduce digital touchpoints smoothly and gain full staff buy-in.

Hospitality staff work under immense physical and mental pressure during busy shifts. If a manager suddenly announces a new piece of technology on a Friday afternoon without explanation, resistance and friction are guaranteed.

1. Involve Shift Leaders in the Selection Process

Before finalizing hardware placements, walk the floor with your head bartender, head chef, and assistant managers. Ask them where the worst bottlenecks occur: Is it ferrying drinks menus to the garden? Waiting for bill splitting? Looking up gluten ingredients?

When staff see that the touchpoint solves a problem that personally exhausts them on shift, they become enthusiastic advocates rather than reluctant users.

2. Never Install During Service Hours

A golden rule of hospitality tech installation: zero technicians on the floor during prep or trading hours. All mounting, alignment, and network verification must happen when the doors are closed. When staff arrive for their pre-service briefing, the touchpoints should already be fitted, cleaned, and fully operational.

3. Keep the Staff Briefing Under 10 Minutes

Because practical NFC and QR touchpoints require zero apps or logins from diners, staff training should be refreshingly simple. Show them how a guest taps their phone, demonstrate how the menu or Google review page loads in two seconds, and explain the phrase to use when seating guests: "Our daily specials and wine list are right on the table marker here with a quick tap of your phone."
