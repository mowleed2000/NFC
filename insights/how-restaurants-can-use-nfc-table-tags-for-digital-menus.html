How Restaurants Can Use NFC Table Tags for Digital Menus

Paper menus get worn, sticky, and obsolete when daily specials sell out. Here is how independent restaurants implement durable table pucks to modernise dining rooms.

Menu reprint cycles cost an average UK restaurant thousands of pounds annually. Even more frustrating are last-minute changes: a supplier delivers different cuts of meat, a craft beer keg blows mid-shift, or seasonal market prices fluctuate.

The Hardware: Table Pucks vs Paper Laminates

Instead of distributing loose paper or sticky laminated folders, restaurants fit low-profile acrylic or solid oak pucks to table corners or beer garden benches. The pucks are permanent, wipeable, and sealed with high-tack commercial adhesives that prevent theft or tampering.

Real-Time Allergen Accuracy

UK hospitality venues must comply strictly with Natasha's Law and comprehensive allergen declarations. Digital menus accessible via table tags allow diners to filter gluten, dairy, nuts, and vegan preferences independently on their phones, avoiding kitchen miscommunications during busy peak shifts.

Preserving the Warmth of Floor Hospitality

Crucially, table tags do not eliminate waiting staff. They free servers from constantly running back and forth to fetch spare printed menus, allowing them to focus on genuine guest hospitality, dish recommendations, and wine pairings.
